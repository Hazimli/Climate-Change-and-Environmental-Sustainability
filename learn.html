01 / THE BASICS

Two ideas.
Often confused.

Climate change describes long-term shifts in climate patterns. Global warming is the long-term heating of Earth's surface. They overlap, but they are not identical.

🌡

Climate change

Long-term changes in average weather patterns at local, regional and global scales. It includes changes beyond temperature, such as rainfall, ice, sea level and extremes.

☀

Global warming

Long-term heating of Earth's surface, driven in the modern era mainly by human activities that increase heat-trapping greenhouse gases.

02 / THE SCIENCE

Follow the
heat.

The natural greenhouse effect makes Earth habitable. The problem is the human-driven increase in greenhouse gases, which strengthens heat retention.

01

Sunlight arrives

Solar energy reaches Earth's surface and atmosphere.

02

Earth warms

The surface absorbs energy and emits infrared radiation.

03

Heat meets gases

Greenhouse gases absorb and re-radiate some outgoing infrared energy.

04

More GHGs

Human activities add gases such as CO₂ and methane to the atmosphere.

05

More warming

More heat is retained in the Earth system, shifting climate conditions.

THE MAIN PLAYERS

Greenhouse gases are not all the same.

Different gases have different sources and warming properties. Water vapour is also a greenhouse gas, but it mainly acts as a feedback rather than the initial driver of today's warming.

CO₂Fossil fuels, deforestation and other activities
CH₄Livestock, landfills, wetlands and fossil-fuel systems
N₂OAgriculture, fertilizers and combustion
CFCsIndustrial chemicals; many are now controlled under international agreements
