01 / WHY IT MATTERS

Climate change is a systems problem.

The atmosphere, oceans, land, living things and human activity are connected. When one part changes, other parts can respond too.

Our mission is to turn complex climate information into something clear, visual and useful — so people can understand the issue and make more responsible choices.
